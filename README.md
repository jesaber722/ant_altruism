Simulation of evolution of ant altruism toward queen. Unlike most vertebrates where both male and female are diploid, male ants are haploid (having only one copy of each chromosome). This has rather large implications in evolutionary terms. For one, a female ant will share more DNA with her full sister (75%) than her own offspring (50%). This incentivizes her to support her queen to have more offspring than to try to have her own. The simulations in the program reflect this, with these genes always becoming dominant.
